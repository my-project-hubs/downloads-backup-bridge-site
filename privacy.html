Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Downloads Backup Bridge ("the app") backs up files to your own Google account after you sign in with Google and grant permission.

Data we access

The app accesses only the Google account data you explicitly authorize, and uses it only to provide the backup feature.

Data sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to any third party. Your files are stored only in your own Google account.

Data retention and removal

You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google Account permissions page. Files in your Google account remain under your control.

Contact

For questions, contact the developer through the Google Cloud project support email.
